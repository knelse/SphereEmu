    ALCHEMICAL REACTIONS FOR EXAMPLE AND TEACHING THE BASICS OF MASTERY
    
    I will tell you here only about a few known reactions, but the full formulation will be reflected in the main work of my life - the Encyclopedia of Cardinal and Fixed Alchemy by Brittan Leonsky.
    So,
    
    CARDINAL REACTIONS
    A simple healing powder. Combine the chatlit stone, the Paris plant and complete the reaction with Fainal powder. Chatlit corresponds to the quality of life and is controlled by the element of water. Paris is a diurnal plant and is also controlled by air. An attentive reader of my work remembers that daytime plants contribute to the preservation of good. The magician conducting the reaction should not forget that the best and correct result will be obtained in the afternoon.
    
    Amilus powder. Combine the ardamant stone and the creatolus flower, completing the reaction with Fainal powder. Ardamant exhibits the quality of order and corresponds to water. Creatolus is a morning plant that corresponds to fire. This is a very rare type of stable reaction in which warring elements are involved.
    
    FIXED REACTIONS
    In order to create a deceleration powder, combine a camelic stone, a fern flower, an iron metal and complete the reaction with Amilus powder. The camelic corresponds to the quality of the force and the element of air, the fern is a nocturnal plant, also controlled by air. The fern contributes to the preservation of evil. Iron contributes to the manifestation of the negative aspect of camelica - weakness. The reaction wastes a significant amount of air from the body of the magician conducting the reaction.
    
    To obtain the incineration powder, combine the stone galeonate, the flower circulonia and the metal iron. Galeonate corresponds to fire and exhibits the quality of death in this reaction under the influence of iron. Circulonia is an evening flower, it is also controlled by the element of fire. The reaction consumes air, and even more so fire from the magician's body.
