03 The great book of mantras
100 The Great Book of mantras or grimoire, as it is called by other magicians, is a magic spell book. Like many other books in the Sphere, it is connected with its owner in a special way - once a magician learns or comes up with a new spell, it immediately appears in the magic book. With the help of a grimoire, a magician can memorize and use many spells, but if he loses or gives his book of mantras to someone, he will instantly lose his witchcraft power and will not be able to cast the simplest spells. It is said that the most powerful wizards can transfer the secret writings of their magic books to specially prepared parchment and even to paper - this is how magic scrolls appear, which are often used by ordinary people who know nothing about magic. Like all other magic books, the grimoire can only save 16 black or white mantras.
