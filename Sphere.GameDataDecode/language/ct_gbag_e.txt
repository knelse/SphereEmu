100 A great bag or a shoulder bag is an ordinary "companion" of an experienced traveler. It costs a lot, and therefore adventurers - from those that are more victorious - are usually content with a small bag. A large bag, made by a good craftsman, is usually made of thick leather, and therefore not only protects the luggage from rain, but also covers the back from a not very strong blow.  In a large bag, you can put 25 pieces of different items - say, bags for magic powders or a neatly folded chain mail. The bag has only one drawback - you can't hang more than one on your shoulders.
