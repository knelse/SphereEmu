03 Jack's appeal.
100 Before you is Jack's appeal to all residents of the Sphere.
TEXT
    I have researched the portal and now I know how to close it, but for this ritual I need to collect 1000 bloody dragon teeth.
    They can be obtained by killing green, blue or red dragons.
    Such a task is beyond the power of any one person, or even a hundred. Only by combining everyone's efforts will we be able to gain so much.
    Do not throw out this message: if I succeed, then this will confirm your participation in the ritual and you will receive a reward!
