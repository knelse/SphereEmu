TEXT
    Centuries ago, the banking system was developed in the Sphere, and bank couriers flew between settlements, using magic for flights and transporting valuables. The reputation of the courier clan was extremely high. One day a merchant, the richest man of that time, decided to move from the city of Bangville to Shipstone. For safety, he hired flying couriers. After learning about this, they were attacked by a gang of robber Noseless. Noseless managed to use magic, which turned out to be no weaker than the magic of couriers. All the robbers and many couriers died in the battle; a huge part of the treasures remained scattered in the rocks, and treasure hunters are still looking for them. But they are hampered by the souls of the dead couriers who fly over the rocks and try to fulfill their duty to protect wealth. Flying skeletons do not always appear, so there is a chance to find something valuable and not meet them. If they appear, an incense stick to ward off perfume and your own sword will help.
    
    Nilus of Nomrad, historian of His Majesty Leon II, King of the Sphere, Duke of Charon, Lord of the six continents.
    May is the month of the year 7421.
