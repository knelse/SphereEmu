1024 <cl=E5B924>Did you know that:<BR>
00 <cl=534322>It is better for a beginner not to leave a safe city, because strong opponents roam in the vicinity. In the beginning, it's worth swinging in the city dungeons. To do this, go to the NPC issuing the mission and click on it with the left mouse button.
01 <cl=534322>The direction of the mission target can be determined using a point that is displayed on the compass. If the mission is in a dungeon, then the goal is indicated only before entering the maze.
02 <cl=534322>You can take a weapon in your hand by clicking on it in the inventory. It will appear in the corresponding slot in the character window (P key).
03 <cl=534322>The game has two cursor modes. In "combat" mode (crosshair in the center of the screen) You can use weapons, in the "normal" (cursor-arrow) - use items in the inventory, trade, etc. Modes are switched by right-clicking on an empty place on the screen.
04 <cl=534322>At the beginning of the game you are given a few thalers. Their number is visible in the inventory (I key) in the first slot at the top left. If you don't have enough thalers, you can earn them by completing NPC missions, selling found items, or exchanging GXP for regular ones in a bank.
05 <cl=534322>Answers to most questions can be found in the help system. It can be called using the F1 key.
06 <cl=534322>To start trading with an NPC or other character, you need to click on it with the left mouse button. At the same time, your character should not have a weapon in his hand. A second click closes the trading windows.
07 <cl=534322>To buy an item in the trade window from an NPC or another player, click on the check mark next to the item. You can't drag items that don't belong to you :)
08 <cl=534322>To heal your character, you need to take a powder or a healing mantra and apply it to yourself (by holding down the Alt key)
09 <cl=534322>To complete the mission to rescue the hostage in the dungeon, you need to find and click on it. After that, the hostage will follow you and you must escort him to the exit of the dungeon, protecting him from danger. Hostages outside the dungeons need to be escorted to the nearest teleport.
10 <cl=534322>You can remove the weapon by pressing the "~" key. Pressing "~" again selects fists as weapons.
11 <cl=534322>The main "hotkeys" in the game are: I - inventory, P - character doll window, H - character characteristics window, K - hotkey settings. The full list of keys can be found in the help (called by F1).
12 <cl=534322>To apply an item in your hand, the cursor must be in "combat" mode.  A simple click applies the object to the target under the cursor, a click with the Alt key pressed on itself.
13 <cl=534322>There are areas with traps in the mazes. Passing through such a zone, the character gets damaged.
14 <cl=534322>There are treatment areas in the labyrinths. Passing through such a zone, you can replenish your health.
15 <cl=534322>Chests in labyrinths are usually protected by traps. Opening such a chest causes damage. You can defuse the trap with a special elixir.
16 <cl=534322>If a bag falls out of the killed monster, click on it with the left mouse button (a window with its contents will appear). Then click on the items you want to pick up from the bag. There should be free space in your inventory.
17 <cl=534322>It is much more convenient to play by tying frequently used items to "hotkeys". They are configured in the window called by the K key. More information about the "hotkeys" can be found in the help (called by F1).
18 <cl=534322>After death, your character will be reborn in the vicinity of the nearest city. Within 30 seconds of respawning, you are invulnerable.
19 <cl=534322>By clicking on another player in the "normal" cursor mode, you open a trading window with him. Drag the items you want to sell into it and enter the price next to the item.
20 <cl=534322>Unlike most games, there are two types of experiences in the Realm world. Killing monsters with cold steel or with a crossbow, you will receive title stamps. If you use magic powders or mantras, you get degree stamps. The levels in the title or degree grow independently of each other as the stamps are received.
21 <cl=534322>The further away from the city, the stronger the monsters. Long-distance travel is dangerous for beginners.
22 <cl=534322>You can get information about any object, character or NPC in the game by holding down Shift and clicking on it.
23 <cl=534322>When you get another title, you get 4 elementals of the title. They can be used to increase strength, agility, accuracy or endurance. Strength is usually needed to use axes, hammers and clubs. Dexterity is for swords. Marksmanship is for crossbows. Endurance is for armor.
24 <cl=534322>When you get another degree, you get 4 degree elementals. They can be used to increase the skill in the elements of earth, air, water or fire. Different powders, mantras and magical things have different requirements for the skill of one of the elements.
25 <cl=534322>Some good things in the game are sold only for GXP. Their number is visible in the "GXP" field above the chat. You can buy GXP on the game's website.
26 <cl=534322>Expensive things bought for GXP do not work until they are "tied" to the owner. To do this, click on the item and confirm the binding. You can untie the item from an NPC named Morrie Vrampel in Torvil.
27 <cl=534322>To use most teleports, you need to have a teleportation token. Click on the token in the inventory while standing on the teleport or drag the token to the teleport.
28 <cl=534322>Chat modes "whisper", "normal speech", "trade" can be quickly switched using the F2, F3 and F4 keys, respectively.
29 <cl=534322>While completing missions, collect items that fall out of monsters and hidden in chests - even if you don't need them, they can be sold to a merchant or other players.
30 <cl=534322>When there are not enough inventory slots, you can buy a larger bag and additional bags for small items. Usually merchants of things for travelers stand near taverns and inns. There can only be one bag and it can only be placed in the second slot of the inventory.
31 <cl=534322>To shoot a crossbow, you need to buy arrows. When they are in the inventory, the crossbow is used as a normal weapon.
32 <cl=534322>The color of the character's name above his head means his karma. Killing other players and defenseless creatures spoils karma. Characters with bad karma are reborn in the most dangerous places on the continent.
33 <cl=534322>The herbs and minerals you find in the game are used for alchemical reactions and the production of magic powders. You can cook powders in a personal pot, but the most powerful ones can only be made in an alchemical furnace.
34 <cl=534322>As you use all the items wear out. Some of them can be repaired at the blacksmith Nib Trempel in Sanpool. Players with a specialty of a gunsmith or a blacksmith can repair those things that they have created, but a limited number of times.
35 <cl=534322>Players can join clans and develop much more efficiently, as well as own castles, wage wars against other clans and form alliances. For high-level characters, some game moments will not be available if he is not in the clan.
36 <cl=534322>There are currently 4 continents available in the world of the Sphere: Hyperion, where new characters start the game; Charon, a gloomy place with volcanoes and glaciers under a red sky; Phoebus, in whose dense tropical forests the most dangerous monsters are hiding; and Rhodes, the haven of the aristocracy.
37 <cl=534322>Any player needs a haven - a room in a tavern with a roomy chest or at least a barn for storing valuable, but rarely necessary things. The room key can be purchased from a merchant in a tavern or on the market square.
38 <cl=534322>You can get to other continents through large teleports. To access, you need to buy a license.
39 <cl=534322>In each city there is a banker NPC, where you can keep your most valuable things or exchange GXP for ordinary ones.
40 <cl=534322>In traveling around the world, you will find castles. When passing through the territory adjacent to the castle, you must pay a small fee to the clan-owner. If you do not pay, you will face Royal Wrath.
41 <cl=534322>To capture the castle, you need to destroy the monsters guarding it, enter the interior and click on the castle tablet. It is almost impossible to capture a castle of your level alone.
42 <cl=534322>Owning a castle allows you to access the castle merchant, the treasury, rooms like in taverns, a special dungeon and much more.
43 <cl=534322>It is much more difficult to develop alone than in a team. Several players can form a group (G key). The group gains experience more efficiently and can take group missions with increased rewards.
44 <cl=534322>When traveling around the world, you can meet special NPC-guilders. After completing their tasks, you can get a specialty or improve your level in the chosen specialty. The specialty modifies the parameters of the character and gives unique skills or spells.
45 <cl=534322>The map is a faithful assistant to the traveler in the world of the Sphere. You can open the map window with the "M" key. The map always shows your location (little man), the city and the purpose of the mission, if any.
46 <cl=534322>The "+" and "-" buttons in the map window control transparency, and the button next to them allows you to switch to the detailed map mode, on which you can arrange your icons and make notes.
47 <cl=534322>At the beginning of the game, you only have a Hyperion community card and a book of cards. Buy more detailed maps from NPCs and put them in the book. To do this, open the character's "doll" (P key), click on the card book (with a compass on the cover) and drag the purchased cards into it.
48 <cl=534322>A player who has reached a position in the game can purchase a personal island with a large house and chests for storing things. The island can be improved many times by getting new opportunities for personal use. There's even an alchemy furnace on a level 10 island!
49 <cl=534322>The character's health is gradually restored by itself, but only if he is full. Satiety can be viewed in the characteristics window (H key). Food is sold in any tavern.
50 <cl=534322>To join a clan, press N and select "join a clan" in the window that appears. Use the changed cursor to click on another player in the clan. After that, he will see your name in the list of applicants in the clan window and will be able to accept you.
51 <cl=534322>Clans that have a castle can declare wars or form alliances (in the clan window). If there is a war between clans, then karma does not deteriorate when killing enemies. Clans in an alliance can visit each other's castles and exchange messages (team /sayally in chat).
52 <cl=534322>The list of special commands can be seen by typing /help in the chat.
