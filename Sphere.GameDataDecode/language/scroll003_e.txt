TEXT
    The traveler!  You have found yourself in the domain of His Majesty Arthur the Fifth - King of the Sphere, Duke of Charon, lord of the six continents.  His Majesty knows that you, a traveler, have arrived from the Earth's Firmament inaccessible to our glorious kingdom.
    Being in the Sphere and coming under the protection of any city belonging to His Majesty, you, traveler, recognize yourself as a subject of the most merciful King Arthur the Fifth. In order not to disturb the peace of good citizens, you need to rent a room at an inn.
    His Majesty wishes you glorious victories over evil spirits, which brazenly annoys his kind subjects.
