100 A small bag or duffel bag is an ordinary "companion" of a beginner or a poorer traveler. It costs much cheaper than a large bag of good work, but there is less benefit from a small bag. It is made of thick cloth and scraps of leather, so you can't carry a lot of weight in it. In a small bag, you can put 8 pieces of different items - for example, bags for magic powders, a helmet or spare shoes. The bag has only one drawback - you can't hang more than one on your shoulders.
