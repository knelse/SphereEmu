100 In front of you is a scroll with a description of the recipe for magic powder. Each recipe hides the painstaking and long-term work of several generations of magicians. In order not to lose such a valuable scroll, save it in a special recipe book.
TEXT
    To get incineration powder, first of all get galleonate, circulonium and iron. Mix them in an alchemical pot, add the final Amilus powder and use the resulting powder. Do not forget that in order to make this reaction, your degree must be no lower than a master's degree.
