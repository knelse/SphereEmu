01 The Eternal Bag
03 the eternal bag
100 The eternal bag is the companion of a very experienced traveler. It costs a lot and therefore adventurers - from those that are more victorious - do without it. Things from the eternal bag do not disappear after death, unless it is money and other bags. Such a bag can be worn along with the usual bag. You can put it only in the inventory cell that is under the money cell.
