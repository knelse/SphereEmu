01 The book of maps
03 a book of maps
100 A long time ago, when people were just settling in the Sphere, the mighty magicians of those times created books of maps. Like all things created by ancient magicians, such books also bear the stamp of magic. It is only necessary to take the map book in hand, as it immediately opens on the right page, showing the traveler exactly the places where he is - unless, of course, there is a suitable map in the book. The books of maps were created so long ago that their magic has become familiar and has not been revered as a miracle for a long time. And the spell needed to create the book is now known not only to sorcerers, but also to simple merchants-cartographers. However, the card book has its flaw - it can only contain 20 cards.
