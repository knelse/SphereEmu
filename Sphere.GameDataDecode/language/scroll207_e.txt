03 Liege Castle
100 In front of you is a scroll with an ancient legend from the world of the Sphere.
TEXT
    In 5236, Lord Stubborn built Liege Castle to protect the road from Bangville to Shipstone from monsters. The castle towers were high and the walls were strong, but this did not help the lord: four years later the castle was captured and looted by monsters. Since then, the lord has tried several times to regain control of the castle, but in the end, the monsters re-captured Liege every time. Liege Castle can be found west of the road leading north from Bangville.
