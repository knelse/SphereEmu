    STONES USED IN CARDINAL AND FIXED ALCHEMY
    There are many stones that can participate in alchemical reactions. Everyone knows that every stone is related to any element: earth, air, water or fire. Your humble servant has also discovered that, in addition to the elements, stones are controlled by the following noble qualities:
    
    Love
    Wisdom
    Ease
    The heat
    Power
    Light
    The order
    Life
    
    When stones are combined with plants and metals, these qualities can manifest themselves in all their power or, on the contrary, turn into their opposites.:
    
    Hate
    Ignorance
    Heaviness
    Cold
    Weakness
    Darkness
    Chaos
    Death
    
    So, the quality of the stone and its element let us know in which direction the reaction will go
    
    ABOUT PLANTS
    Alchemical science knows many plants. Like stones, every plant has its own related element. In addition, it has been known for a long time that any flower or grass acquires the greatest strength at a certain time of the day. Therefore, we divide plants into night, morning, afternoon and evening.
    
    Night herbs and flowers contribute to the preservation of evil.
    Morning ones contribute to the birth of both evil and good.
    Daytime preserve the good.
    Evening ones destroy both good and evil.
    
    Everyone knows that by creating a reaction during the day with a day plant, we always get the right and predictable result. In the evening or in the morning, you can get an alchemical explosion. And at night, the daytime plant should not be used at all: almost always the result will be deplorable. Do the same with morning, evening and night plants. The further the Sun is from the position needed by the plant, the worse and more dangerous the consequences.
    
    I have established that when a stone and a plant are combined, the related elements of these relate to each other in different ways.
    
    The earth is strengthened by the earth, is friendly with water, neutral to fire and hostile to air.
    The air is strengthened by air, friendly with fire, neutral to water and hostile to earth.
    Water is strengthened by water, is friendly with the earth, neutral to air and hostile to fire.
    Fire is enhanced by fire, friendly with air, neutral to earth and hostile to water.
    
    There are almost always bad consequences from the combination of hostile elements, however, there are very rare cases when it is possible to maintain a balance and combine two hostile elements. For example, you can recall the recipe for Amilus powder, combining vajdebnye water and fire.
    
    So, to make cardinal reactions, a stone and a plant are enough, which are lowered into the alchemical pot in a strict sequence: first the stone, then the plant. The reaction is completed with Fainal powder. Let us especially note that the Fainal powder always brings out the positive aspect of the stone, be it life, power, light and others.
    
    ABOUT METALS
    Metals allow for fixed transformations. Here I will describe only five basic metals that can be used in an alchemical cauldron. These are gold, silver, copper, iron, lead.
    
    Gold corresponds to the golden age of our Sphere and, participating in the reaction, most painfully reveals the positive aspect of the stone.
    Silver corresponds to the silver age of our Sphere and weakly reveals a positive aspect.
    Copper reflects the copper age of the Sphere and is a stabilizer. Sometimes copper reveals a very weak positive aspect.
    Iron reflects the iron age of the Sphere and weakly reveals the negative aspect of the stone, be it hatred, cold, death and others.
    The lead corresponds to the time when the last days will come to the Sphere. Lead most fully reveals the negative aspect of the stone.
    
    So, for the completion of fixed reactions, a stone, a plant, a metal and an Amilus powder are needed, which are lowered into the alchemical cauldron in strict sequence: first the stone, after the plant, after the metal and the powder.
