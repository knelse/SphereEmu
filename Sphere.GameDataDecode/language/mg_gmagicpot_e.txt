100 In a great alchemy pot, you can boil twice as many powders as in a normal one. The alchemical cauldron was first created in 1008 by the court magician of King Valentine - Amilus. Before his invention, bulky alchemical furnaces were used, occupying entire houses. The pot also made it possible to carry out most of the necessary alchemical transformations in field conditions. Only complex and expensive reactions still required furnaces. For many centuries, the art of building furnaces has been lost, and the number of working furnaces has been reduced due to wars and cataclysms.  In the modern Sphere, not a single whole alchemical furnace is known. If someone finds a working furnace, he will be able to carry out very rare and valuable transformations.
