TEXT
    The unicorn is an unusual creature that appeared after the beginning of Transformations in the Sphere 5-6 millennia ago. He spends most of his life in the gap between the worlds, so he is invisible.  Only sometimes the path leads the Unicorn to one world, then to another, then to a third. Once a year, the Unicorn sheds its horn, which also remains in everlost. The horn is used to create a ring of invisibility. Many centuries ago, Noviz, the Master of the Hall of the Remaining, managed to get the horn using the shaking of the worlds. The horn simply fell out into the Sphere during the concussion, and a dead glade remained at the site of the concussion. Subsequently, Rester's fern was used to extract the horn. With the help of a magical Eye created from fern and quartz, you can highlight, manifest the horn and drag it into your world.
    
    Nilus of Nomrad, Master of Magic, court alchemist and historian of His Majesty Leon II, King of the Sphere, Duke of Charon, Lord of the six continents.
