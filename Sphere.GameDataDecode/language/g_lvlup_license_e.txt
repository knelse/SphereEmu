03 license to increase the level of specialty
100 Increases the level of specialization of the character by 1. To increase the level by 1 step, you need as many licenses in the stack as the level to which the specialization increases. For example, to go from level 3 to level 4, you need 4 licenses in a stack. There are no additional bonuses available for completing the relevant tasks. At the same time resets the distributed characteristics of the character.
