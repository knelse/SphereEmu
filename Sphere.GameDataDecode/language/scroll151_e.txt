100 In front of you is a scroll with a description of the recipe for magic powder for cooking in the oven. Each recipe hides the painstaking and long-term work of several generations of magicians. In order not to lose such a valuable scroll, save it in a special recipe book.
TEXT
    To get lightning powder, first of all get pyrite, cerden and iron. Mix them in the oven, melt the oven with brushwood and use the resulting powder. Do not forget that in order to make this reaction, your degree must be no lower than a master's degree.
