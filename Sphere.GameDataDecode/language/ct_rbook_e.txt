03 recipe book
100 The recipe book is the most important book in the life of an alchemist magician. Like many other books in the Sphere, this is a magical book - it is connected by a special bond with its owner. No matter what mysterious composition he mixed in his alchemical cauldron, a record of the ingredients and the result of the reaction will appear in the book by itself. This has happened since ancient times - the fact is that alchemical experiments are by no means safe. Therefore, sometimes only a torn and burned recipe book could tell students and colleagues what fate befell an unsuccessful magician. However, it must be remembered that only up to 20 entries can be entered into the recipe book - this number is determined by the measure of the magic power that the master put into the book when creating it.
